Questions

Is it really free?
Yes. A free account shows 10 bets a day, at least 15 minutes behind, with no card. Pro and Premium show every bet, live, and start with 7 days free. Cancel any time.
Does Kismet place bets for me?
No. Kismet shows bets; you decide and place them yourself on Polymarket, or not at all. The bets are picked the same way for everyone: it's market analytics, not advice.
What does “wins about 7 in 10” mean?
Out of 10 past bets like this one, about 7 won. It comes from how similar markets settled. It's a guide, not a promise.
Can I lose money?
Yes. Any single bet can lose, including good-value ones, and past results don't promise future ones. Only bet what you can afford to lose.
Who can use it?
Adults 18+ in the United States. Polymarket US isn't open in every state, so check your local rules before you bet.
